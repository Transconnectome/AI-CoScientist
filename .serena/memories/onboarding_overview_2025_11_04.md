As of Nov 4 2025 the AI-CoScientist repo consists of a FastAPI backend with async SQLAlchemy, Redis, Celery, and ChromaDB-backed RAG services under src/. API routers live in src/api/v1, business logic in src/services (llm orchestration, knowledge base, paper scoring, RAG manager), and multi-agent coordination in src/router and src/agents. Config is handled via src/core/config.py with .env-based settings. There is a React/Vite frontend scaffold in frontend/. Docker Compose defines postgres, redis, chromadb, api, celery worker/beat. Tests are organized under tests/ with unit, integration, and e2e suites. Documentation is centralized in docs/ (INDEX, ARCHITECTURE, DEVELOPMENT, DEPLOYMENT) and QUICK_START.md covers setup.
