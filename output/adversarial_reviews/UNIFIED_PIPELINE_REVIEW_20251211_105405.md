**Link:** To secure funding, the proposal must address these issues by including detailed plans for control groups, robust statistical analyses, realistic impact assessments based on preliminary data, and comprehensive documentation to ensure reproducibility. Without these, the project's feasibility and potential for successful implementation remain questionable.
