#### Pilot 2: Simulated Quantum Circuit (2-qubit toy problem) (2-3일)

**What:**
- 2큐비트 Quantum Classifier (Iris dataset, 150 샘플)
- 목표: "우리는 양자 회로를 실행할 수 있다"는 증명 (기술적 능력)

**How:**
```python
# Use Qiskit Aer (local GPU)
from qiskit import QuantumCircuit, Aer, execute
from qiskit.circuit.library import ZZFeatureMap, RealAmplitudes

# 2-qubit variational classifier
feature_map = ZZFeatureMap(feature_dimension=2, reps=1)
ansatz = RealAmplitudes(num_qubits=2, reps=1)
circuit = feature_map.compose(ansatz)

# Train on Iris (2 features, 2 classes)
backend = Aer.get_backend('qasm_simulator')
result = execute(circuit, backend, shots=1024).result()

# Expected: 85-90% accuracy (Iris는 쉬운 데이터셋)
```

**Deliverable:**
- **Figure 2:** "2-qubit Quantum Classifier on Iris Dataset: Classical (92%) vs. Quantum (88%)"
