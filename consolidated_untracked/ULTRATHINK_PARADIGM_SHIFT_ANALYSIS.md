- **Time to build:** 3-5 years

- **Time to build:** 5-10 years
- **Example:** Nature/Science journals (prestige moat = 150+ years old)

- **Mechanism:** Fixed infrastructure costs spread over millions of users
- **Quantification:** 90% gross margins at scale (vs. 40% for competitors)
