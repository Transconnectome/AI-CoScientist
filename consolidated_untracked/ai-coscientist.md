This skill provides comprehensive scientific research capabilities using the AI-CoScientist system for paper review, grant proposal writing, and research analysis.

- **Paper Review**: Comprehensive analysis with multi-expert evaluation
- **Grant Proposal Enhancement**: QuantERA, NSF, NIH proposal optimization
- **Literature Analysis**: Advanced RAG-based research synthesis
- **Statistical Validation**: Rigorous methodology and results analysis
