## TASK

Review and refine the attached peer review for manuscript MS 37480, focusing on:

### 1. Terminology Accuracy
**Critical Issue:** The review uses **"NPP 2025 standards"** as if it's an official guideline, but this is actually the reviewer's analysis of recent NPP publications.

**Fix Required:**
- Replace "NPP 2025 standards" with appropriate language:
  - "Recent NPP publications indicate..."
  - "Contemporary expectations in Neuropsychopharmacology..."
  - "Analysis of recent NPP papers (2024-2025) suggests..."
  - "Marek & Laumann (2025, NPP 50:52-57) recommend..."

**Never imply:**
- Official journal guidelines that don't exist
- Formal policy where there's only emerging practice
- Mandatory requirements that are actually recommendations

### 2. Professional Tone
**Check for:**
- Overly harsh or dismissive language
- Condescending tone toward authors
- Excessive certainty where uncertainty exists
- Inappropriate informal language
- Over-use of emphasis (bold, italics, capitals)

**Ensure:**
- Constructive, collegial tone throughout
- Recognition of authors' expertise
- Balance between critique and acknowledgment
- Professional distance (not personal)

### 3. Evidence-Based Claims
**Verify:**
- Every citation is accurate and appropriate
- Claims about "standards" are properly attributed
- Statistical thresholds are justified
- Comparisons to other work are fair

**Red flags:**
- "Standard practice" without citation
- "Always required" when it's actually variable
- "NPP requires" when it's editorial preference
- Absolute statements without nuance

### 4. Recommendation Clarity
**Ensure:**
- Clear distinction between:
  - ESSENTIAL (must address for acceptance)
  - STRONGLY RECOMMENDED (should address)
  - OPTIONAL (nice to have)
- Realistic feasibility of recommendations
- Acknowledgment of what's addressable with existing data vs. requiring new studies

### 5. Structural Issues
**Check:**
- Appropriate section organization
- No redundancy across sections
- Logical flow of arguments
- Clear summary of main points

### 6. Specific Phrases to Revise

**Instead of:** "NPP 2025 standards require..."
**Use:** "Recent NPP publications increasingly expect..." OR "Marek & Laumann (2025) recommend..."

**Instead of:** "This violates Tier 2 standards"
**Use:** "This falls below contemporary expectations for..." OR "Recent high-impact studies have included..."

**Instead of:** "NPP 2025 emphasis"
**Use:** "Recent editorial priorities in NPP..." OR "Barch & Liston (2025 editorial) emphasize..."

**Instead of:** "Tier 1/2/3 criteria"
**Use:** "Mandatory requirements" / "Standard expectations" / "Excellence indicators" (but explain these are YOUR framework, not official NPP)

**Instead of:** "Below NPP 2025 standard"
**Use:** "Below sample sizes typically seen in recent NPP publications" OR "Smaller than the N≥100 recommended by Marek & Laumann (2025)"

### 7. Maintain Strengths
**Keep:**
- Evidence-based reasoning with citations
- Specific, actionable recommendations
- Recognition of study strengths
- Constructive suggestions for improvement
- Comprehensive coverage of all dimensions
