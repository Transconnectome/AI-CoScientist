**Goal:** Show QFF achieves convergence where standard backprop fails

```python

# Benchmark: Which converges?
```

**Expected Results:**
- Baseline (SPSA): Loss plateaus at ~0.5 after 200 iterations (stuck)
- QFF: Loss decreases to <0.1 within 300 iterations (converges)
