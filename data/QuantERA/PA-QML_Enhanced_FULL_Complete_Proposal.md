## Enhanced Summary of the Project (Publishable Abstract)

**Physics-Aware Quantum Machine Learning (PA-QML) establishes the foundational framework for distributed quantum computing through progressive Multi-QPU coordination protocols and reliability-certified quantum algorithms.**

**Challenge:** Current Quantum Machine Learning remains confined to small-scale demonstrations with untrainable ansätze that fail at scale. No existing approach addresses the fundamental scalability barriers preventing practical quantum advantage in real-world applications.

**Innovation:** We pioneer the paradigm shift from "heuristic QML" to "Physics-Aware & Scalable QML" through two primary breakthroughs: (1) **Progressive Multi-QPU Coordination**—developing hierarchical distributed quantum computing starting with classical ensemble aggregation and progressively introducing selective quantum correlations where demonstrable advantage exists; (2) **Hybrid QFF-HQGA Optimization**—integrating Quantum Forward-Forward algorithms with Hybrid Quantum Genetic Algorithms providing dual-path optimization with graceful classical degradation.

**Validation Strategy:** Rather than claiming immediate quantum supremacy, we establish validated theoretical frameworks through staged validation: 2-QPU classical coordination → selective quantum enhancement → full multi-QPU demonstration on IBM Quantum systems. Each phase includes rigorous classical baselines ensuring objective quantum advantage assessment.

**Enhanced Consortium:** Our international partnership combines Seoul National University's multi-chip ensemble expertise, University of Naples' quantum computational intelligence leadership, Fraunhofer IKS's industrial certification authority, Yonsei University's CERN collaboration experience, and IBM Research's quantum hardware partnership providing guaranteed cloud access and practical validation pathways.

**Expected Impact:** This project transforms QML from experimental demonstrations to preliminary engineering validation, establishing Europe-Asia leadership in practical distributed quantum machine learning while training the next generation of quantum algorithm engineers for industry deployment.

## Enhanced Relevance to the Topic Addressed in the Call

**This project directly addresses QuantERA's call to "explore novel quantum phenomena and resources" and "address major challenges preventing broad applications" through:**

**Novel Quantum Phenomena Exploration:**
- **Collective Quantum Advantage:** We establish new theoretical understanding of how quantum correlations can be selectively introduced across distributed quantum processors to achieve ensemble-based quantum advantage
- **Quantum-Classical Hybrid Optimization:** We explore the quantum-classical boundary through QFF-HQGA dual-path optimization, determining when quantum methods provide advantage over classical approaches
- **Hardware-Native Quantum Logic:** We investigate how physical quantum noise can be repurposed as a computational resource through Fuzzy Quantum Logic and POVMs

**Addressing Major Application Challenges:**
- **Scalability Barrier:** Our Multi-QPU coordination protocols address the fundamental limitation that quantum advantage has been restricted to small-scale systems by developing distributed approaches that aggregate quantum resources
- **Trainability Crisis:** Our QFF-HQGA optimization resolves the barren plateau problem that prevents deep quantum circuits from being trainable, enabling practical quantum machine learning
- **Reliability Gap:** We develop preliminary certification frameworks addressing the critical lack of safety standards for quantum ML systems, essential for industrial deployment

**Quantum Resources Development:**
- **Multi-QPU Resource Theory:** We establish mathematical foundations for distributed quantum computing resource allocation and optimization
- **Progressive Quantum Enhancement:** We develop methodologies for incrementally introducing quantum advantage while maintaining classical fallback mechanisms
- **Hardware-Aware Algorithm Design:** We create quantum algorithms specifically designed for current NISQ constraints rather than idealized quantum computers

**European Strategic Alignment:**
This project strengthens European quantum technology leadership through the Fraunhofer IKS partnership, Munich Quantum Valley integration, and European Quantum Computing Benchmarking Committee coordination, while establishing critical Asia-Europe-USA quantum research bridges through IBM Research partnership.

**Broad Application Foundation:**
We validate our approaches across three strategic domains: High Energy Physics (CERN LHC data), Neuroscience (clinical EEG/fMRI), and Cybersecurity (network intrusion detection), demonstrating quantum algorithm applicability across Europe's priority research and industrial sectors.
