## PART 4: RISK ASSESSMENT MATRIX

### Risk Categories & Mitigation Strategies

#### TECHNICAL RISKS

**Risk 1: Pilot studies fail to show quantum advantage**
- **Likelihood:** 20-30% (moderate)
- **Impact:** HIGH (undermines grant proposal core claims)
- **Mitigation:**
  - Run multiple experiments with different parameters
  - Focus on easier benchmarks (MNIST, not complex datasets)
  - Lower success threshold (+3-5% gain, not +7-8%)
  - Use simulation (Qiskit Aer) if QPU access fails
- **Fallback:**
  - Reframe proposal as "exploratory research" (not "proven concept")
  - Focus on theoretical contributions (QFF formulation)
  - Emphasize infrastructure value (DD-RAPTOR system)
- **Contingency Cost:** -1.0 to -1.5 points proposal score

**Risk 2: Platform fails product-market fit validation**
- **Likelihood:** 30-40% (moderate-high)
- **Impact:** HIGH (platform path becomes non-viable)
- **Mitigation:**
  - Interview 50+ researchers before launch (validate demand)
  - Create multiple use case templates (broad appeal)
  - Offer freemium tier (reduce adoption friction)
  - Partner with universities for institutional access
- **Fallback:**
  - Pivot to "research-as-a-service" (consulting model)
  - License technology to existing platforms (Elicit, Semantic Scholar)
  - Focus on niche domain (quantum research only)
- **Contingency Cost:** $50K-100K (development costs sunk)

**Risk 3: Code quality/documentation insufficient for open-source**
- **Likelihood:** 15-25% (low-moderate)
- **Impact:** MEDIUM (delays platform launch, reduces adoption)
- **Mitigation:**
  - Allocate 2 weeks for refactoring (not 1 week)
  - Hire technical writer for documentation (if budget allows)
  - Beta test with 10 users before public launch
  - Create video tutorials (easier than written docs)
- **Fallback:**
  - Closed beta for 1-2 months (invite-only)
  - Partner with early adopters for onboarding support
  - Gradual open-sourcing (release modules incrementally)
- **Contingency Cost:** 2-4 week delay

#### RESOURCE RISKS

**Risk 4: Team bandwidth insufficient for dual-track execution**
- **Likelihood:** 40-50% (high)
- **Impact:** CRITICAL (both tracks suffer, neither succeeds)
- **Mitigation:**
  - Pre-assess team availability (explicit commitment)
  - Hire contractor/intern for non-critical tasks (documentation, testing)
  - Reduce scope: 2 pilots instead of 3, MVP instead of full platform
  - Clear prioritization: Grant > Platform (if forced to choose)
- **Fallback:**
  - Abandon hybrid strategy, choose single path (Path A or B)
  - Delay platform launch by 2-3 months (post-grant submission)
  - Outsource specific tasks (website design, video editing)
- **Contingency Cost:** 2-4 week delay OR single-path pivot

**Risk 5: Funding for compute/QPU access unavailable**
- **Likelihood:** 10-20% (low)
- **Impact:** HIGH (cannot run pilots, grant proposal weakened)
- **Mitigation:**
  - Confirm IBM Quantum Network access before starting
  - Budget €10K for AWS Braket backup
  - Use simulation (Qiskit Aer) if QPU unavailable
  - Partner with institution with QPU access
- **Fallback:**
  - Theoretical-only proposal (no preliminary data)
  - Focus on platform differentiation (not grant)
  - Delay submission to 2026 (acquire access first)
- **Contingency Cost:** -2.0 points proposal score (no prelim data)

#### MARKET RISKS

**Risk 6: QuantERA competition more intense than expected**
- **Likelihood:** 50-60% (high, uncontrollable)
- **Impact:** HIGH (funding probability lower than projected)
- **Mitigation:**
  - Target score 7.8+/10 (not just 7.3/10)
  - Add optional improvements (external review, letters of support)
  - Differentiate through platform innovation angle
  - Prepare alternative funding applications (backup plans)
- **Fallback:**
  - Resubmit to QuantERA 2026 (with stronger credentials)
  - Apply to alternative grants (INCITE, Samsung, EIC)
  - Pivot fully to platform (if grant path unviable)
- **Contingency Cost:** 1-year delay + opportunity cost

**Risk 7: Platform market becomes saturated (AI research tool competition)**
- **Likelihood:** 30-40% (moderate, accelerating)
- **Impact:** HIGH (harder to differentiate, raise funding)
- **Mitigation:**
  - Launch quickly (first-mover advantage)
  - Focus on specific niche (quantum + medical AI, not general)
  - Build moats: Proprietary algorithms, unique datasets, network effects
  - Strategic partnerships (IBM, Google, pharma) for distribution
- **Fallback:**
  - Pivot to B2B enterprise (not B2C researchers)
  - White-label licensing (infrastructure for others)
  - Acquisition target (sell to Elsevier, Springer Nature, etc.)
- **Contingency Cost:** Lower valuation, harder fundraising

#### EXTERNAL RISKS

**Risk 8: Grant timeline too tight (deadline <4 weeks away)**
- **Likelihood:** UNKNOWN (check deadline immediately)
- **Impact:** CRITICAL (insufficient time for 4-week sprint)
- **Mitigation:**

- **Likelihood:** 10-15% (low, but high impact)
- **Impact:** CRITICAL (project continuity threatened)
