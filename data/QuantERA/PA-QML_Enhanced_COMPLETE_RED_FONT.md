## <span style="color:red">Enhanced Summary of the Project (Publishable Abstract)</span>

<span style="color:red">**Physics-Aware Quantum Machine Learning (PA-QML) establishes the foundational framework for distributed quantum computing through progressive Multi-QPU coordination protocols and reliability-certified quantum algorithms.**</span>

**Challenge:** Current Quantum Machine Learning remains confined to small-scale demonstrations with untrainable ansätze that fail at scale. No existing approach addresses the fundamental scalability barriers preventing practical quantum advantage in real-world applications.

**Innovation:** We pioneer the paradigm shift from "heuristic QML" to "Physics-Aware & Scalable QML" through two primary breakthroughs: (1) <span style="color:red">**Progressive Multi-QPU Coordination**—developing hierarchical distributed quantum computing starting with classical ensemble aggregation and progressively introducing selective quantum correlations where demonstrable advantage exists</span>; (2) <span style="color:red">**Hybrid QFF-HQGA Optimization**—integrating Quantum Forward-Forward algorithms with Hybrid Quantum Genetic Algorithms providing dual-path optimization with graceful classical degradation</span>.

<span style="color:red">**Validation Strategy:** Rather than claiming immediate quantum supremacy, we establish validated theoretical frameworks through staged validation: 2-QPU classical coordination → selective quantum enhancement → full multi-QPU demonstration on IBM Quantum systems. Each phase includes rigorous classical baselines ensuring objective quantum advantage assessment.</span>

<span style="color:red">**Enhanced Consortium:** Our international partnership combines Seoul National University's multi-chip ensemble expertise, University of Naples' quantum computational intelligence leadership, Fraunhofer IKS's industrial certification authority, Yonsei University's CERN collaboration experience, and IBM Research's quantum hardware partnership providing guaranteed cloud access and practical validation pathways.</span>

<span style="color:red">**Expected Impact:** This project transforms QML from experimental demonstrations to preliminary engineering validation, establishing Europe-Asia leadership in practical distributed quantum machine learning while training the next generation of quantum algorithm engineers for industry deployment.</span>

<span style="color:red">**Targeted Breakthroughs**</span>

<span style="color:red">[RED TEXT MODIFICATIONS INDICATED]</span>

This project targets <span style="color:red">**two fundamental barriers**</span> preventing Quantum Machine Learning (QML) from achieving practical advantage, while establishing a robust foundation for future quantum computing applications. Current QML remains confined to small-scale demonstrations reliant on heuristic ansätze that become untrainable at scale. We shift the paradigm from "heuristic QML" to <span style="color:red">**"Physics-Aware & Scalable QML"**</span>—algorithms designed for NISQ constraints <span style="color:red">**with rigorous theoretical foundations and practical validation pathways**</span>.

<span style="color:red">**Primary Breakthrough 1 – Progressive Multi-QPU Coordination:**</span> We develop <span style="color:red">**a hierarchical approach to distributed quantum computing, starting with classical aggregation of independent QPU outputs and progressively introducing quantum correlations where demonstrable advantage exists**</span> [1]. Our "Selective Entanglement" protocol introduces inter-chip quantum connections only for globally-dependent features <span style="color:red">**identified through classical mutual information analysis**</span>. Classical ensemble methods provide algorithmic error mitigation with <span style="color:red">**variance reduction ∝ 1/k, establishing a practical pathway toward "Collective Quantum Advantage" that scales with available hardware**</span>. This architecture aligns with emerging modular quantum hardware platforms (e.g., IBM Quantum, IonQ, Pasqal), enabling direct deployment as interconnected QPU systems become available.

<span style="color:red">**Primary Breakthrough 2 – Hybrid Optimization with Graceful Degradation:**</span> We integrate Quantum Forward-Forward (QFF) [2] with Hybrid Quantum Genetic Algorithm (HQGA) [3] <span style="color:red">**as a dual-path optimization strategy. QFF's local goodness objectives provide mathematical advantages in avoiding barren plateau concentration of measure [4], while HQGA offers gradient-free global search when QFF proves insufficient. This hybrid approach ensures robust optimization with classical fallback mechanisms, reducing project risk while exploring novel quantum advantages**</span>.

<span style="color:red">**Secondary Objective 3 – Advanced Temporal Modeling (Stretch Goal):**</span> <span style="color:red">**Contingent on success of primary breakthroughs, we explore**</span> Quantum State Space Models (Q-SSM) [5] integrating three-branch quantum superposition with LSTM-style gating. <span style="color:red">**This component will be pursued only after establishing solid foundations in Years 1-2, with classical alternatives available if quantum approaches prove insufficient.**</span>

<span style="color:red">**Secondary Objective 4 – Noise-Resilient Generation (Future Work):**</span> <span style="color:red">**Building on primary successes, we investigate**</span> Fuzzy Quantum Logic via POVMs [10] for hardware-native noise modeling. <span style="color:red">**This represents an advanced research direction contingent on demonstrating core capabilities first.**</span>

### 1.3 Concept and Methodology

<span style="color:red">**Overall Concept: Hardware-Aware QML Stack**</span>

We assume NISQ devices remain fragmented and noisy <span style="color:red">**for the project duration**</span>. Rather than waiting for fault tolerance, we redesign algorithms to <span style="color:red">**exploit current constraints while providing clear scaling paths for future hardware improvements**</span>.

<span style="color:red">**Core hypothesis:**</span> Quantum advantage lies in <span style="color:red">**feature representation efficiency combined with ensemble robustness**</span>, not error-free computation. Our hybrid architectures extract quantum benefits via <span style="color:red">**validated shallow circuits**</span> while delegating complex temporal processing to <span style="color:red">**proven classical components**</span>.

<span style="color:red">**Enhanced Methodology**</span>

<span style="color:red">**M1: Progressive Multi-QPU Coordination (→O1: Scalability)**</span>

<span style="color:red">**Phase 1 - Classical Foundation (M1-M6):**</span>
Establish <span style="color:red">**classical ensemble baseline**</span> using k independent quantum models processing <span style="color:red">**correlated data partitions**</span>. Validate variance reduction ∝ 1/k without quantum correlation overhead.

<span style="color:red">**Phase 2 - Selective Enhancement (M6-M18):**</span>
Introduce <span style="color:red">**quantum correlations selectively**</span> for feature pairs showing <span style="color:red">**demonstrated classical correlation >0.7**</span> (mutual information threshold). Measure <span style="color:red">**incremental quantum advantage**</span> over classical ensemble baseline.

<span style="color:red">**Phase 3 - Scaling Validation (M18-M24):**</span>
Demonstrate <span style="color:red">**multi-QPU coordination**</span> on <span style="color:red">**IBM cloud**</span> and <span style="color:red">**Yonsei physical hardware**</span>. Validate <span style="color:red">**practical implementation**</span> with realistic noise and connectivity constraints.

<span style="color:red">**Scientific basis:**</span> <span style="color:red">**Staged validation approach**</span> eliminates risk of claiming quantum advantage without classical comparison. <span style="color:red">**Hardware partnership**</span> ensures practical implementability rather than theoretical speculation.

<span style="color:red">**M2: Dual-Path QFF-HQGA Optimization (→O2: Optimization)**</span>

<span style="color:red">**QFF Implementation with Safety Nets:**</span>
Deploy QFF <span style="color:red">**local goodness objectives**</span> [2] with <span style="color:red">**mathematical verification**</span> of gradient variance properties. <span style="color:red">**Continuous monitoring**</span> of convergence behavior with <span style="color:red">**automatic fallback**</span> to HQGA when improvement plateaus.

<span style="color:red">**HQGA as Robust Alternative:**</span>
Quantum genetic algorithms with <span style="color:red">**entangled crossover**</span> provide <span style="color:red">**gradient-free global search**</span> when local optimization fails. <span style="color:red">**Classical initialization**</span> and <span style="color:red">**quantum enhancement**</span> rather than pure quantum evolution.

<span style="color:red">**Integration Protocol:**</span>
1. <span style="color:red">**Classical pretraining**</span> to establish favorable initial conditions
2. <span style="color:red">**QFF local optimization**</span> with convergence monitoring
3. <span style="color:red">**HQGA global search**</span> when QFF improvement <10⁻⁴ for 10 consecutive iterations
4. <span style="color:red">**Performance comparison**</span> against established VQA baselines

<span style="color:red">**Scientific basis:**</span> <span style="color:red">**Dual-path approach**</span> eliminates single-point-of-failure risk. <span style="color:red">**Quantified switching criteria**</span> ensure objective performance evaluation rather than subjective assessment.

# PA-QML Enhanced Executive Summary

## Physics-Aware Quantum Machine Learning: From Distributed Hardware to Certified Intelligence
<span style="color:red">**QuantERA 2025 Enhanced Proposal - Executive Abstract**</span>

<span style="color:red">**Transformative Vision:**</span> We pioneer the paradigm shift from "heuristic QML" to "Physics-Aware & Scalable QML"—establishing the first validated framework for distributed quantum machine learning that scales with emerging hardware while maintaining rigorous theoretical foundations.

<span style="color:red">**Critical Problem:**</span> Current Quantum Machine Learning remains trapped in small-scale demonstrations plagued by barren plateaus and untrainable ansätze. No existing approach addresses the fundamental scalability barriers preventing practical quantum advantage in real-world applications.

<span style="color:red">**Revolutionary Breakthroughs:**</span>

<span style="color:red">**Primary Breakthrough 1 - Progressive Multi-QPU Coordination:**</span> We develop the first hierarchical distributed quantum computing architecture, beginning with classical ensemble aggregation and progressively introducing quantum correlations where demonstrable advantage exists. Our "Selective Entanglement" protocol introduces inter-chip quantum connections only for globally-dependent features identified through classical mutual information analysis. This provides variance reduction ∝ 1/k while establishing a practical pathway toward "Collective Quantum Advantage" that scales with available hardware.

<span style="color:red">**Primary Breakthrough 2 - Hybrid Optimization with Graceful Degradation:**</span> We integrate Quantum Forward-Forward (QFF) with Hybrid Quantum Genetic Algorithm (HQGA) as a dual-path optimization strategy. QFF's local goodness objectives provide mathematical advantages in avoiding barren plateau concentration of measure, while HQGA offers gradient-free global search when QFF proves insufficient. This hybrid approach ensures robust optimization with classical fallback mechanisms, reducing project risk while exploring novel quantum advantages.

<span style="color:red">**Enhanced Consortium Strength:**</span> Our internationally distributed team combines Seoul National University's multi-chip ensemble expertise, University of Naples' evolutionary algorithm leadership, Fraunhofer IKS's certification authority, Yonsei University's CERN collaboration experience, and IBM Research's quantum hardware partnership providing guaranteed cloud access and in-kind hardware expertise.

<span style="color:red">**Realistic Validation Strategy:**</span> Rather than claiming immediate quantum supremacy, we establish validated theoretical frameworks through progressive scaling: 2-QPU classical coordination → selective quantum enhancement → full multi-QPU demonstration. Each phase includes rigorous classical baselines ensuring objective performance evaluation.

<span style="color:red">**Risk-Resilient Implementation:**</span> Our staged approach eliminates single-point-of-failure risks through dual-path optimization, classical pretraining phases, and automatic fallback protocols. A 6-month pilot phase with €50K preliminary funding validates proof-of-concept before full project launch.

<span style="color:red">**Practical Impact Pathways:**</span> We validate on genuine complexity datasets while maintaining realistic scope: HEP particle correlation tasks with classical baselines, preliminary neuroscience applications on moderate-complexity EEG/fMRI data, and robustness certification framework development. This transforms QML from experimental demonstrations to preliminary engineering validation.

<span style="color:red">**Industrial Relevance:**</span> Our hardware-aware design philosophy assumes NISQ devices remain fragmented and noisy throughout the project duration. Rather than waiting for fault tolerance, we redesign algorithms to exploit current constraints while providing clear scaling paths for future hardware improvements. This practical approach ensures immediate applicability to existing quantum systems.

<span style="color:red">**Budget Optimization:**</span> €1.45M strategically allocated across 36 months with flexible reallocation mechanisms: core team maintenance, hardware access scaled to demonstrated capabilities, and staged deliverables ensuring primary breakthroughs validation before secondary objectives.

<span style="color:red">**Measurable Success Criteria:**</span> ≥90% accuracy retention at 2× QPU scaling with measurable quantum advantage in feature correlation tasks; convergence at >6 layers where classical methods fail with ≥30% efficiency improvement; statistical quantum advantage (p<0.05) in domain validation metrics.

<span style="color:red">**European Leadership Position:**</span> This project establishes Europe as the global leader in practical distributed quantum machine learning, providing validated methodologies for the emerging quantum computing industry while training the next generation of quantum algorithm engineers.

<span style="color:red">**Long-term Vision:**</span> We lay the foundational infrastructure for the quantum machine learning stack of the 2030s, when multi-QPU systems become commercially available. Our progressive coordination theory and hardware-aware algorithms provide the critical bridge from today's experimental systems to tomorrow's practical quantum advantage.
