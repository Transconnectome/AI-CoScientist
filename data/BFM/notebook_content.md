## 1. Executive Summary: The "AI Scientist" Vision
The core objective is to transcend current LLM limitations ("Pattern Alchemy") and achieve **"Reasoning as a Phase Transition"** by integrating biological brain mechanisms with large language models. The ultimate goal is to build an **Integrated Multimodal Brain Foundation Model (155B parameters)** that can serve as a "Digital Twin Brain" for medical diagnosis and scientific discovery.

## 2. Key Research Documents & Proposals

### A. Research Proposal: LLM Alignment-based Multimodal Brain Foundation Model
**Goal:** Construct a 155B parameter model integrating MRI, EEG, and Multi-omics.

#### **Phased Development Roadmap**
1.  **Phase 1: 92B Multimodal Brain Neuroimaging Model**
    - **Architecture:** Vision Transformer + SwiFT (Swin 4D fMRI Transformer).
    - **Data:** fMRI, sMRI, dMRI.
2.  **Phase 2: 125B Integrated Brain-Imaging-EEG Model**
    - **Architecture:** DIVER (Deep Integrated Variational Encoder-decoder?) architecture.
    - **Integration:** Adding high-temporal resolution EEG data to spatial MRI data.
3.  **Phase 3: 155B Comprehensive Brain-Omics-LLM Model**
    - **Methodology:** "Emergent Alignment" to fuse biological signals (Omics) with linguistic/logic representations.
4.  **Phase 4: Clinical Application**
    - **Target:** Deployment for cognitive reserve prediction and dementia transition analysis.
    - **Metric:** Goal of 7% accuracy improvement over SOTA.

#### **Risk Management**
- **Stability:** Strategies for Mixture of Experts (MoE) stability.
- **Reliability:** Mitigating hallucination in clinical reports using RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) and RLHF.

### B. Technical Whitepaper: Self-Evolution Framework for LLM Reasoning
**Concept:** Moving from static pre-training to dynamic "Self-Evolution" (Generate → Verify → Learn).

#### **Evolution Strategy**
1.  **Data Evolution (Smart Data > Big Data)**
    - **Synthetic Data Generation (SDG):** Using smaller "Teacher Models" to generate high-quality training data.
    - **Curriculum Filtering:** Targeting the "sweet spot" of difficulty (20-80% success rate) to maximize learning efficiency.
    - **Interactive Red-Teaming:** AI-assisted stress testing.

2.  **Model Evolution**
    - **RLVR (Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards):** Internalizing reasoning steps (Chain-of-Thought) into the model weights.
    - **Outcome:** Significantly reduced inference overhead compared to prompting-only reasoning.

## 3. Key Concepts & Theories

### **Edward Chang: Coordination Layer (System-2)**
- **Critique:** Current LLMs are "System-1" (Pattern Alchemy)—fast but prone to error.
- **Proposal:** Add a "Coordination Layer" (System-2) to manage and verify patterns.
- **Theory:** Reasoning is a **Phase Transition** that occurs when "Semantic Anchoring" exceeds a specific threshold (critical mass of verifiable knowledge).

### **Yejin Choi: Unconventional Innovation**
- **Thought-Tracing:** Explicitly tracking the mental state changes during reasoning.
- **Democratization:** Using non-traditional algorithms/data to allow smaller models to reason as well as larger ones.
- **Scientific Application:** Apply thought-tracing to explain *why* an experiment yielded specific results (Causal reasoning).

### **Bradley Love: BrainGPT**
- **finding:** LLMs can predict neuroscience experiment results better than human experts.
- **implication:** LLMs have successfully "internalized" the patterns of scientific literature.
- **Next Step:** Use this predictive power to create an "Hypothesis-Verifying Agent" (Automated Peer Review & Experimental Design).

## 4. Synthesis: The "Digital Twin Brain"
- **Concept:** Cross-Modal Phase Transition.
- **Mechanism:** Use BrainGPT (Text/Knowledge) as a "Semantic Anchor" for Neuro-X (Physical/Signal Data).
- **Goal:** Detect the exact threshold where a model "understands" a neurological pattern, enabling the "decoding" of human thought from brain signals (1.2M people, 666TB scale).
