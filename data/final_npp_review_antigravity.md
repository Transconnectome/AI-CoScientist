Confidential remarks for the Editor/Publisher:
The authors have executed a highly responsive and rigorous revision, effectively addressing the most critical concerns regarding overfitting and validation. While a sample size of N=54 remains below the ideal threshold for robust biomarker identification (as highlighted by the Marek & Laumann citation added by the authors), the study utilizes a **drug-naïve** cohort, which is exceptionally difficult to recruit and scientifically valuable. The authors have successfully reframed the work as "exploratory" and "hypothesis-generating" rather than definitive validation. They implemented rigorous Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV) with permutation testing to establish a null distribution, addressing the statistical inflation concerns. The comparison against clinical models and the addition of Decision Curve Analysis demonstrate potential clinical utility. Rejecting the manuscript now would be moving the goalposts, as the authors have met the high bar set by the reviewers (particularly Reviewer 1). The paper represents a technically sound, well-caveated step forward in the search for OCD biomarkers.

Remarks to be sent to the author:
The authors are to be commended for a thorough and scientifically rigorous revision.
